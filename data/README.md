# data/ — your starting data

This folder is your fixed input. **The portfolio is synthetic; the sources are real.**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `partners.json` | The **synthetic** SME portfolio — 22 fictional electronics companies, 53 products, every contact an `@example.com` placeholder. This is the fixed dataset you assess. A few companies carry a `compliance_status` block (`certs_held` + `known_gaps`) — treat those as **ground truth** your assessment should agree with. |
| `taxonomy.json` | Controlled vocabulary: product categories, substances, and regulation families (with their EU legal references). |
| `SOURCES.md` | The curated list of **real official sources** — EUR-Lex, ECHA, EPREL, national EPR registers, EU Safety Gate — to pull current requirements from. **Start here for the FIND step.** Includes the "which source for which rule" quick map. |
| `sample_expected_output.json` | The exact shape of **one** finding your agent should emit. Your `findings/findings.json` is a list of records like this. |

## Two folders you'll create as you build

- **`data/cache/`** — commit the raw responses you fetch in the FIND stage here, each with
  its `source_url` and access date. This is the evidence that your ingestion is real. (It's
  **not** git-ignored — commit it.)
- **`findings/`** (repo root) — where your emitted output lands.

## Integrity note

`partners.json` is generated by a maintainer script and is **intentionally fictional** — it
must not be read as data about any real company. The regulations you assess against are real
and public; cite their official source URLs. See the root
[`README.md`](../README.md#about-this-proov-project) for the full not-affiliated disclaimer.
